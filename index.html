How to Play Arkeon

  1. How to Place or Swap a Piece (3 Easy Steps):
    • Step 1: Click/tap a unit card in your Unit Arsenal.
    • Step 2: Click/tap any green dashed hex on the board to lock your target hex.
    • Step 3: Use Rotate ⟲ / ⟳ (or keys Q / E) to aim your yellow laser beams, then click Confirm Move ✓ (or click the same hex a second time).
  2. Lasers & Friendly Fire: Your unit fires lasers into the adjacent hex(es) it points at—destroying any unit in those spaces, including your own friendly units!
  3. Supply-Line Rule (Base Connection): Every unit must keep a continuous chain of adjacent friendly units back to its Home Base. Any unit cut off from its base (marked with CUT!) is immediately destroyed.
  4. 5-Unit Advanced Pool & Recycling: Basic Strikers are infinite. You have 5 Advanced Units. Downgrading an Advanced unit on the board back into a Basic Striker returns the Advanced piece to your hand. Destroyed Advanced units go to your Spent pool; once all 5 are destroyed, all 5 refresh!

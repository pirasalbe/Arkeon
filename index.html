How to Play Arkeon: Complete Rules

  1. How to Make a Move (3 Steps):
    • Step 1: Select an enabled unit card from your Unit Arsenal. Any unit that currently has zero legal moves on the board is automatically disabled and marked No Valid Moves.
    • Step 2: Click or tap any green dashed hex on the board to lock your target hex and preview your yellow laser beams.
    • Step 3: Use Rotate ⟲ / ⟳ (or press Q and E) to aim your lasers, then click Confirm Move ✓ (or click the same hex a second time).
  2. Turn Actions (Deploy vs. Replace / Swap Units):
    • Deploy a New Unit: Place a unit from your reserve onto an empty green hex adjacent to at least one of your existing units.
    • Upgrade a Unit: Select an Advanced unit in your Arsenal, then click one of your existing Basic Strikers on the board to replace it. The Basic Striker returns to your infinite supply.
    • Downgrade a Unit: Select the Basic Striker in your Arsenal, then click one of your existing Advanced units on the board to replace it. The removed Advanced unit returns directly to your available hand so you can redeploy it elsewhere on a later turn.
    • Swap Advanced Units: You can also replace an Advanced unit on the board with a different Advanced unit from your reserve, returning the previous one to your hand.
    • No In-Place Rotation: You cannot spend a turn simply rotating a unit already on the board; you must replace it with a different unit type.
  3. When Do You Get Spent / Cut Advanced Units Back?
    • Basic Striker (Infinite Supply): Fires 1 laser beam. Destroyed Basic Strikers are always reusable.
    • 5 Advanced Units: Spear V, Wide Fork, Lancer Line, Trident Fan, and Tri-Star.
    • Destroyed or Cut Units Go to Spent Pool: When an Advanced unit is destroyed on the board (either by a direct laser HIT! or isolated by a supply-line CUT!), it is marked as Spent and cannot be used until your pool refreshes.
    • All-5 Refresh Rule: Once all 5 of your Advanced units have been destroyed or cut (5/5 Spent), all 5 immediately refresh and return to your available hand at the same time. (Tip: To reuse an Advanced unit without waiting for all 5 to die, downgrade it to a Basic Striker on your turn before the enemy destroys it.)
  4. Lasers, Guarded Hexes & Friendly Fire:
    • Guarded Hexes (No Stepping Into Enemy Lasers): Any empty hex currently pointed at by an enemy unit's laser is an active kill zone. You cannot deploy a unit onto a hex that is in the line of fire of an enemy laser, even if you try to aim back at that enemy unit. To destroy an enemy unit, you must flank it from an unguarded adjacent hex or upgrade an existing unit outside its firing line.
    • Mandatory Friendly Fire: When placed or swapped, a unit immediately fires into the adjacent hex(es) it points at, destroying any unit in those spaces, including your own friendly units. You can use friendly fire intentionally to clear your own frontline and open new attack lanes.
  5. Supply-Line Rule (Base Connection):
    • Every unit on the board must maintain an unbroken chain of adjacent friendly units connected back to its Home Base (RED HQ at C1, BLUE HQ at C7).
    • If a laser strike destroys a link in that chain, all disconnected units (enemy or friendly, marked with CUT!) are destroyed immediately on the same turn.
    • You cannot make a suicidal move that leaves your newly placed unit disconnected from your Home Base, and you cannot shoot your own Home Base.
  6. Enemy Base Restriction, Stalemates & Winning the Game:
    • Enemy HQ Approach Rule: You cannot place or swap a unit on a hex directly adjacent to the opponent's Home Base unless that unit is aiming a laser directly at the enemy Home Base.
    • Victory Conditions: You win immediately by firing a laser at the enemy Home Base, eliminating all enemy units, or leaving your opponent with no legal moves.
    • Passive Swap Limit (6 Swaps): If 6 consecutive turns (3 per player) consist only of passive unit swaps without deploying onto an empty hex or destroying an enemy unit, the stalemate ends automatically and the player controlling more units on the board wins (or a draw is declared if tied).

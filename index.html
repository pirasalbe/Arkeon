How to Play Arkeon

  1. How to Place or Swap a Piece (3 Easy Steps):
    • Step 1: Select a unit card from your Unit Arsenal (Basic Striker is selected automatically).
    • Step 2: Tap any green highlighted hex on the board. Empty green hexes deploy a new unit; green hexes containing your own unit upgrade/downgrade (swap) that piece.
    • Step 3: Use the Rotate ⟲ / ⟳ buttons to aim your yellow laser beams, then tap Confirm Move ✓ (or tap the same hex a second time).
  2. Directional Lasers & Friendly Fire: When confirmed, your unit fires lasers into the adjacent hex(es) it points at—destroying any unit in those spaces, including your own friendly units!
  3. Supply-Line Rule (Base Connection): Every unit must keep a continuous chain of adjacent friendly units back to its Home Base. Any unit cut off from its base (marked with a red CUT! badge during preview) is immediately destroyed.
  4. 5-Unit Advanced Pool & Recycling: Basic Strikers are infinite. You have 5 Advanced Units. If you downgrade an Advanced unit on the board back into a Basic Striker, the Advanced unit returns straight to your hand. Destroyed Advanced units go to your Spent pool; once all 5 are destroyed, all 5 refresh automatically!
  5. Winning the Match: Aim any unit's laser directly at the enemy Home Base, or eliminate all enemy units.

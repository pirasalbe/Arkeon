How to Play Arkeon: Official Rules

  1. How to Make a Move (3 Steps):
    • Step 1: Select an enabled unit card from your Unit Arsenal. Any unit that currently has zero legal moves on the board is disabled and marked No Valid Moves.
    • Step 2: Click or tap any green dashed hex on the board to lock your target hex and preview the board outcome (`HIT!`, `CUT!`, `ROT`, or `PUSH`).
    • Step 3: For directional units (Basic Striker and Triple Striker), use Rotate ⟲ / ⟳ (or press Q and E) to aim, then click Confirm Move ✓ (or click the same hex a second time).
  2. Turn Actions (Deploy vs. Replace / Swap Units):
    • Deploy a New Unit: Place a unit from your reserve onto an empty green hex adjacent to at least one of your existing connected units.
    • Upgrade a Unit: Select an Advanced unit in your Arsenal, then click one of your existing Basic Strikers on the board to replace it.
    • Downgrade a Unit: Select the Basic Striker in your Arsenal, then click one of your existing Advanced units on the board to replace it. The removed Advanced unit returns directly to your available hand so you can redeploy it elsewhere on a later turn.
    • Swap Advanced Units: Replace an Advanced unit on the board with a different Advanced unit from your reserve, returning the previous one to your hand.
  3. The Authentic Unit Arsenal (1 Basic + 5 Advanced Units):
    • Basic Striker (`∞` Supply, Aggressive): Attacks the 1 hex it points at.
    • Triple Striker (`Le triple attaque`, Aggressive): Attacks the 3 forward hexes it points at simultaneously.
    • Clockwise Rotator (`Tournicoteur horaire`, Non-Aggressive): Forces all adjacent aggressive units (both enemy and friendly) to pivot 60 degrees clockwise.
    • CCW Rotator (`Tournicoteur anti-horaire`, Non-Aggressive): Forces all adjacent aggressive units (both enemy and friendly) to pivot 60 degrees counter-clockwise.
    • Pacifier (`Le pacificateur`, Non-Aggressive): Pacifies all adjacent aggressive units (both enemy and friendly) by forcing them to pivot so their lasers point directly away from the Pacifier.
    • Repulsor (`Le répulseur`, Non-Aggressive): Pushes all units (including friendly units and Home Bases) outward by 1 space along all 6 hexagonal axes until they hit the board edge or another blocked piece.
  4. 5-Step Chain-Reaction Resolution Order:
    • Step 1 (Manipulator Effect): If a Rotator, Pacifier, or Repulsor is placed, all rotations or pushes happen first. (A manipulator can safely step into an enemy laser if its effect turns or pushes that laser away!)
    • Step 2 (Active Player's Attacks): All of the active player's aggressive units that were just placed or rotated/pushed fire their lasers.
    • Step 3 (Enemy Supply Check): Any opponent units disconnected from their Home Base are destroyed (`CUT!`).
    • Step 4 (Opponent's Triggered Attacks): Any surviving opponent units that were rotated or pushed in Step 1 now fire their lasers in their new direction (often destroying their own teammates!).
    • Step 5 (Active Player's Supply Check): Any of the active player's units disconnected from their Home Base are destroyed (`CUT!`). A move is illegal if the newly placed unit itself does not survive to the end of Step 5.
  5. The 5-Unit Refresh Rule & Victory Conditions:
    • All-5 Refresh: Destroyed Advanced units enter your Spent pool. Once all 5 of your Advanced units are destroyed, all 5 refresh and become playable again.
    • Victory: You win immediately if an enemy Home Base is hit by a laser (checked in resolution order if both bases are targeted on the same turn) or if your opponent has no legal moves left.

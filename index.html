How to Play Arkeon

  1. Turn Actions (Deploy or Upgrade/Downgrade Swap):
    • Deploy: Place a unit from your Unit Arsenal onto an empty green hex connected to your supply line.
    • Upgrade / Downgrade (Swap): Select a different unit type in your Arsenal and click one of your existing units on the board to replace it. (Rotating an existing unit without swapping its type is not allowed.)
  2. Line of Fire & Counter-Attacks: You cannot end your placement in the active laser beam of a surviving enemy unit. To place on a hex guarded by an enemy laser, your newly placed unit must simultaneously destroy that enemy unit (either by aiming a laser back at it or by severing its supply chain)!
  3. Lasers & Friendly Fire: When confirmed, your unit fires lasers into the adjacent hex(es) it points at—destroying any unit in those spaces, including your own friendly units!
  4. Supply-Line Rule (Base Connection): Every unit must keep a continuous chain of adjacent friendly units back to its Home Base. Any unit cut off from its base (marked with CUT!) is immediately destroyed.
  5. 5-Unit Advanced Pool & Recycling: Basic Strikers are infinite. You have 5 Advanced Units. Downgrading an Advanced unit on the board back into a Basic Striker returns the Advanced piece straight to your hand. Destroyed Advanced units go to your Spent pool; once all 5 are destroyed, all 5 refresh!

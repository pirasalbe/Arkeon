ARKEON — Official Tactical Dynamics

Origins: Designed by Dominique Breton (originally prototyped as Viral) and published by Hyperfiction (2011), Arkeon blends the directional geometry of Chess with the supply-line territory isolation of Go.

1. The 29-Hex Arena & Starting Setup

The board consists of 29 playable hexes situated between the Red Base at the top (0, -3) and the Blue Base at the bottom (0, 3). Each player starts with 3 Basic units deployed on the 3 hexes directly in front of their home base.

2. Turn Actions: Deploy or Upgrade/Downgrade

Players alternate turns performing one action:

  • Deploy: Place a Basic or available Advanced unit from your reserve onto an empty hex connected to your supply line.
  • Upgrade / Downgrade (Swap): Replace one of your existing units on the board with a different type from your reserve. Upgrading a Basic unit to an Advanced unit lets you unleash multi-directional attacks from surprise angles. Downgrading an Advanced unit on the board back into a Basic unit immediately returns that Advanced unit to your available hand so you can redeploy it elsewhere!

3. Indiscriminate Lasers & Supply-Line Isolation (BFS)

  • Immediate Attack & Friendly Fire: When placed or swapped, a unit immediately fires into the adjacent hex(es) indicated by its directional pointers. Any unit—enemy or friendly—in those spaces is destroyed.
  • Base Supply Chain: Every unit on the board must maintain an unbroken chain of adjacent friendly units back to its Home Base. After attacks resolve, any units cut off from their Home Base are instantly isolated and destroyed!
  • Self-Preservation Rule: You cannot make a placement that destroys your own Base, nor one where your newly placed/swapped unit cuts off its own sole connection to its Base.

4. Reserve Recycling Pool (The 5 Advanced Units)

Basic units have an unlimited supply (destroyed Basic units return directly to your hand). Each player also commands 5 unique Advanced units (Spear V, Wide Fork, Lancer, Trident, and Aegis). When an Advanced unit is destroyed (by combat or isolation), it moves to your Spent Pool. Once all 5 of your Advanced units have been destroyed, all 5 immediately refresh back into your available reserve!

5. Victory Condition & Base Adjacency Rule

You win immediately by firing a laser directly into the opponent's Home Base, or by eliminating all enemy units on the board. Note: You may only place a unit on the 3 hexes directly adjacent to the Enemy Base if that unit is oriented to fire into the Enemy Base for the winning strike.
